Workflow

From raw entries to research decisions.

ChronicleUX supports the full diary study lifecycle, from setup and collection to machine-assisted interpretation and evaluator review.

  1. 1

    Create a diary study

    Define the study goal, hypotheses, participant instructions, entry frequency, and research context.

  2. 2

    Collect diary entries

    Participants submit entries describing what happened, how they felt, what worked, and what caused friction. Evaluators can also import from external sources.

  3. 3

    Evaluators analyze entries

    Evaluators use machine-generated summaries, sentiment trends, dominant themes, and detected issues to support decision-making.
